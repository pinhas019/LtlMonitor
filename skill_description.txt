Skill: AutonomousNavigation
Description: The robot navigates to a target location while avoiding obstacles until the target is reached.
============================================================
Execution Phases:
============================================================

[PlanningAndMoving]
  Condition  : nav_accepted_or_executing and not near_target
  Description: The robot is executing the path plan and moving towards the target.

[ApproachingTarget]
  Condition  : nav_accepted_or_executing and near_target
  Description: The robot is in the final stages of approach to the target.

============================================================
Terminal Conditions:
  SUCCESS : nav_success
            The navigation status explicitly reports success.
  FAILURE : nav_failure
            The navigation status reports cancellation or abortion.
